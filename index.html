Aus Granny Flat
Professional Modular Home Design & Manufacturing

Aus Granny Flat is a design and manufacturing enterprise integrating modular home design, production and sales services. Supported by a mature supply chain network and partnered modern intelligent manufacturing factories, we deliver high-quality granny flats, standardised modular dwellings and customised complete building solutions for all local Australian residential property clients.

Core Services

One-stop modular housing solutions from design to delivery

Standardized Products

Multiple mature floor plans, ready to order and produce, including one-bedroom, two-bedroom, and three-bedroom units, ranging from 15 to 80 square meters, with complete specifications and transparent pricing.

Personalized Customization

Professional architects tailor design solutions based on your land, needs, and budget. From floor plan planning to material selection, every detail is exclusively crafted for you.

Factory Manufacturing

Relying on a mature supply chain system and reliable manufacturing factories, modern production lines ensure precise control at every stage. Indoor manufacturing is not affected by weather, with stable quality and controllable progress, and each stage undergoes strict quality inspection.

Transportation & Installation

Professional teams handle transportation and on-site installation, with fastest delivery in 6-8 weeks from order to delivery, and most projects completed efficiently.
